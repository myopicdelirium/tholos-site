The Invisible Hand, Watched

Twenty-six self-interested foragers, scattered on an empty world, with no plan and no coordinator — and the order that assembles itself anyway.

    A single deterministic run (seed 3, 2,400 ticks) on the Batch A substrate with foraging memory enabled. Each forager remembers only where it has eaten well and returns there; nothing broadcasts, nothing commands. Deaths, births and settlements are read straight from the tick where they occur. The reddening of a dot is its own energy failing; the gold line is whichever individual leaves the most descendants. What structure appears — communities on every patch, an empty waste between them, a population held steady for ~190 ticks without a single death — no agent intended.
